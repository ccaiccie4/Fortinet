What is covered today

  • FortiGate: SD-WAN health-check evidence such as state(alive), packet loss, latency, and jitter, and how to move from probe health to rule, policy-route, firewall/NAT/session, and application evidence.
  • FortiManager: validation versus installation versus FortiGate runtime, including the operational meaning of devices being skipped when validation errors exist.
  • FortiAnalyzer: FortiView as an analytics layer over received logs, why a gray/empty dashboard can mean the required log type is missing, and how to trace the gap back through logging and ingestion.
  • FortiSwitch: MCLAG peer/ICL requirements, redundancy expectations, and why healthy unicast does not prove LACP/VLAN/STP or multicast state.
  • FortiAP: client troubleshooting across association, authentication, DHCP/IP, RF/SNR, roaming, and post-IP forwarding, using documented signal/SNR evidence.
  • FortiNAC: device discovery and profiling, why an unknown MAC can legitimately enter the host database as rogue, and how to distinguish profiling state from malicious-device conclusions.
  • FortiAuthenticator: EAP-TLS policy, certificate trust, realms/identity, and authorization as separate troubleshooting stages rather than one generic RADIUS result.
  • FortiPAM: privileged-access policy, Proxy Mode, Session Recording, approval/permission, target reachability, credential validity, and audit/session evidence.
  • FortiSIEM: Supervisor/Worker/Collector roles and why a missing incident may be an ingestion/parsing problem before it is a correlation-rule problem.
  • FortiSASE: private-access ZTNA dependencies across FortiClient/endpoint posture, FortiSASE policy, FortiGate access proxy, and private-resource reachability.
  • Evidence interpretation: each evidence treatment explicitly separates observation, valid inference, non-inference, and the next check.
  • Quiz self-containment: evidence-dependent questions visibly include command/source context, evidence excerpt, exact Fortinet source, and interpretation feedback inside the same question card.

Source/evidence note: command and workflow interpretations are tied to exact Fortinet sources. Evidence excerpts are kept concise and are not invented; if Fortinet does not publish a canonical output, the workflow must say so instead of fabricating one.
